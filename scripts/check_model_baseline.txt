# The eleven axioms below are asserted on terms outside the triple: namespace, and
# each one restates what the source vocabulary itself declares — verified on
# 2026-08-10 against datacite, litre, oa, dcat, adms, sioc, dcterms and schema.org.
# Repeating them keeps each iteration readable and testable on its own.
#
# Anything else fails scripts/check_model.py: a range or a domain that NARROWS a
# foreign property belongs on the class, as an owl:allValuesFrom restriction.
http://purl.org/dc/terms/provenance rdfs:range http://purl.org/dc/terms/ProvenanceStatement
http://purl.org/spar/datacite/hasIdentifier rdfs:range http://purl.org/spar/datacite/Identifier
http://purl.org/spar/datacite/usesIdentifierScheme rdfs:domain http://purl.org/spar/datacite/Identifier
http://purl.org/spar/datacite/usesIdentifierScheme rdfs:range http://purl.org/spar/datacite/IdentifierScheme
http://rdfs.org/sioc/ns#topic rdfs:subPropertyOf http://purl.org/dc/terms/subject
http://www.w3.org/ns/adms#representationTechnique rdfs:range http://www.w3.org/2004/02/skos/core#Concept
http://www.w3.org/ns/dcat#bbox rdfs:range http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#Literal
http://www.w3.org/ns/dcat#theme rdfs:subPropertyOf http://purl.org/dc/terms/subject
http://www.w3.org/ns/oa#motivatedBy rdfs:range http://www.w3.org/ns/oa#Motivation
https://schema.org/contactPoint rdfs:range https://schema.org/ContactPoint
https://schema.org/spatialCoverage rdfs:range https://schema.org/Place
