Abstract

The TRIPLE Ontology defines a comprehensive model for representing Social Sciences and Humanities (SSH) resources in the GoTriple discovery platform, extending Schema.org and FOAF.

1. Introduction

GoTriple is an advanced multilingual discovery platform specifically designed for the Social Sciences and Humanities (SSH) sector. The platform aggregates, processes, and semantically enriches diverse SSH resources including scholarly publications, research datasets, project descriptions, and researcher profiles from various European language repositories. The TRIPLE ontology serves as the data model for this platform, facilitating cross-disciplinary and cross-linguistic discovery and reuse of resources.

Document c back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://gotriple.eu/ontology/triple/Document

A document in the GoTriple platform, representing scholarly publications, research datasets, and other SSH research artifacts. It is a subclass of both schema:CreativeWork and foaf:Document. Beyond the internal id, the GoTriple PID and the identifier from the source system, a document optionally carries a DOI, a Handle, an ISSN or an ISBN, and its landing page, full text and source URLs, each distinguished by its identifier scheme.

Alternative Labels:
